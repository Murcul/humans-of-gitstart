As the Technical Product Team Lead, you will oversee our product development efforts, working closely with a Product Manager and Designer. Your primary responsibility will be to ensure the successful delivery of our existing product roadmap and drive the team towards achieving our goals. This position is a 6-month engagement to cover for maternity leave.

## Reponsibilities

Report to CEO

As part of this role you will:

### Team Collaboration:
- Partner with the Product Manager and Designer to drive product development.
- Foster a positive and productive team environment.
- Ensure the team is organized and efficient to meet project deadlines.

### Roadmap Management:
- Oversee the successful delivery of our existing product roadmap.
- Adjust the scope and reprioritize roadmap items as needed in response to evolving business needs.

### Stakeholder Communication:
- Report directly to the CEO, providing regular updates on product progress, challenges, and successes.
- Collaborate with stakeholders in Client Engineering and Remote Engineering to ensure new projects and features align with team and company goals.
- Work with Engineering to ensure solutions are technically sound and delivered on time.

### Active Product Usage and Dogfooding:
- Lead the team in actively using our app to identify areas for improvement and ensure a high-quality user experience.
- Utilize in-house GitStart teams as clients to accelerate development efforts.
- Continuously monitor metrics to identify and address areas for improvement.

## You have
- Proven experience as a Technical PM Lead
- Excellent leadership and team management skills.
- Strong understanding of product lifecycle, software development lifecycle
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, especially in an async culture

## **Hackweek Based recruiting**

We are weird about hiring. We’re skeptical of resumes and we don’t trust interviews (we’re happy to talk though!). Since nothing beats working together, we love inviting people for a hackweek (or hackweekend) - come hack on a problem with us and see if you like it!

It’s compensated, of course. And fast: we typically extend offers within 3 working days, starting from the end of the hackweek.
